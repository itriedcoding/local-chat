Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

HackerGPT is designed so that your data stays on your device. This policy explains what the application stores, where it is stored, and the limited circumstances in which it makes a network request. It also describes this website.

In short: HackerGPT has no account system, no analytics, and no tracking. Your conversations never leave your computer. The app only reaches the network when you explicitly browse or download models from Hugging Face, or talk to an Ollama server you run.

1. Data stored on your device

HackerGPT writes the following files into your Windows user data folder, under %APPDATA%\hackergpt\:

  • settings.json — your generation and runtime settings (system prompt, temperature, context size, Ollama URL, and similar).
  • models.json — the list of models you have added, including file paths.
  • chats.json — your conversations, with the most recent 300 retained.
  • models/ — the GGUF model files you download through the app.

These files are for the app's operation only. They are not transmitted to us or to anyone else. You can remove all of it at any time by deleting that folder; the Settings tab also offers a "delete all chats" action.

2. Network connections the app makes

HackerGPT does not phone home. It initiates network connections only in these situations:

  • Hugging Face (huggingface.co). When you use the Discover tab, the app queries the public Hugging Face API to search for GGUF models and list their files, and downloads the specific file you choose. These requests go to Hugging Face and are subject to Hugging Face's privacy policy. The app sends only the search terms and model identifiers needed for the request — not your conversations.
  • Your Ollama server. If you use Ollama features, the app sends your prompts to the Ollama server URL configured in Settings (by default http://127.0.0.1:11434 on your own machine). If you point it at a remote host, that host receives your prompts. This is your configuration and your responsibility.

Apart from these, the app does not contact any server. In particular, HackerGPT itself contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or telemetry code.

3. Model outputs

All inference is performed locally by llama.cpp or by the Ollama server you configured. Your prompts and the generated responses are processed on your hardware and are not sent anywhere by HackerGPT.

4. Children's privacy

HackerGPT is a general-purpose developer and hobbyist tool. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because we do not collect personal information at all.

5. This website

This website is a set of static pages. It does not use cookies, and it does not include third-party advertising or tracking scripts. It is served by a hosting provider (Vercel), which may keep standard server logs — such as IP address and request time — for security and operational purposes, as is typical for any web host.

6. Third-party services

Where the app or these pages link to third-party services — Hugging Face, Ollama, GitHub — those services have their own privacy policies that we do not control. Please review them.

7. Data deletion

Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To erase everything locally, close the app and delete %APPDATA%\hackergpt\.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Continued use of the Software after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

Privacy questions can be raised via the project's issue tracker at github.com/itriedcoding/HackerGPT.
